# EV Charging Landscape in the United States

## Public Charging Points
As of **January 2025**, the United States hosts **approximately 195,874 public charging ports** spread across about 69,679 stations. This figure reflects a steady growth trend, with roughly 1,000 new public chargers added each week. The distribution includes a mix of Level 2 AC chargers and DC fast chargers, enabling fleets to plan routes and charging times with greater confidence.

## Major Charging Networks
### 1. Tesla Supercharger Network
- **Ports**: ~35,682
- **Coverage**: Over 2,500 Supercharger stations nationwide, many strategically placed along major interstates.
- **Features**: Rapid DC fast charging (up to 250 kW), proprietary connector, seamless integration with Tesla fleet management software, and priority access for Tesla vehicles.
- **Fleet relevance**: Ideal for fleets operating Tesla vehicles; offers predictable performance and dedicated routing.

### 2. Electrify America
- **Ports**: ~5,350
- **Coverage**: 1,500+ stations across 33 states, with a high concentration in the Midwest and Northeast.
- **Features**: High‑power DC fast chargers (up to 350 kW), open connector (CCS), and a unified billing platform that supports fleet management and reporting.
- **Fleet relevance**: Excellent for non‑Tesla electric fleets; provides interoperability with most OEMs and robust data analytics.

> **Evidence Limitations**: The port counts are based on publicly available data from January 2025 and may have changed since. Network coverage and speeds can vary by location. Fleet operators should verify current station status and connectivity before deployment.
